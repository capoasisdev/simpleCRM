Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 • Effective immediately

This Privacy Policy applies to the SimpleCRM mobile application and web platform ("App", "Service"), owned and operated by CapOasis ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Overview of the Service

SimpleCRM is a customer relationship management and lead management platform designed for businesses to organize, track, and follow up with their sales leads gathered from authorized marketing channels, website forms, and messaging integrations.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

  • Account Information: When you register or log in, we collect your name, email address, company name, and authentication credentials.
  • CRM Lead & Contact Information: Information about prospects and leads that you or your integrated channels import into the CRM. This may include names, phone numbers, email addresses, campaign IDs, and custom form response fields.
  • Device & App Diagnostics: Information about your device, including device model, operating system version, app version, IP address, and crash reports to maintain app security and stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely to deliver and improve our CRM services, specifically to:

  • Facilitate real-time lead ingestion and pipeline tracking.
  • Provide push notifications and reminders for incoming sales leads and follow-ups.
  • Monitor Meta Ads account budget, balance, and campaign spend within your CRM dashboard.
  • Authenticate users and enforce multi-tenant account access controls.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical or security issues.

4. Third-Party Integrations & Service Providers

SimpleCRM integrates with trusted third-party services to deliver core functionality. These partners process data strictly on our behalf under confidentiality agreements:

  • Supabase: Cloud database infrastructure for secure data storage and real-time syncing.
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook & Instagram Lead Ads): Used to retrieve leads generated through your authorized Facebook Pages and Instant Lead Forms.
  • WhatsApp Cloud API: Used to enable direct business messaging and notifications.
  • Resend / SMTP Providers: Used to dispatch transactional notifications and client email communications.
Notice on Third-Party Data: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data or your leads' contact information to third parties or data brokers.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption. Database access is strictly governed by Row Level Security (RLS) policies.

We retain your personal information and CRM records for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the Service.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion Requests

You have the right to access, rectify, export, or permanently delete any personal information associated with your account.

If you wish to delete your account or request the complete removal of your business data and leads, you may do so at any time by contacting us:

7. Children's Privacy

Our Services are directed exclusively to business professionals and are not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date above.
